Bot Development2026

Maungpedia Bot

A full-stack WhatsApp bot built for managing pilot training services. Handles login/logout tracking, automated reminders, and email distribution.

The bot manages the entire workflow for a pilot training service. Users send commands through WhatsApp to log in and out of their sessions, and the bot records timestamps, calculates durations, and stores everything in a structured JSON database.

It also manages email distribution lists, sends scheduled reminders, and can export all service records into formatted Excel spreadsheets with proper date formatting. There's a web dashboard built with Express that shows real-time session data and connection status.

On top of that, it has a Discord integration that sends alerts when the WhatsApp connection drops and posts daily summaries. To handle multiple users sending commands at the same time, there's a sequential command queue with a 2-second delay that prevents race conditions during file writes.

Chinvoice

An AI-powered multi-branch invoice and receipt management system with intelligent vision OCR for handwritten Indonesian notes.

Web App2026

Account Vault

A client-side encrypted password manager. All data stays in your browser, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Everything runs in the browser with zero external dependencies. When you set a master password, it goes through PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations to derive an encryption key, then all your accounts are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a fresh random IV every time you save.

There's no backend, no tracking, and no way for anyone (including me) to access your data. For cross-device sync, it optionally connects to GitHub Gist where only the encrypted ciphertext blob is stored. The UI has categories, search, keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+S to save, Esc to close), and a glassmorphism dark/light theme.

Bot Development2025

Discord Payment Notifier

A Discord bot that monitors incoming payment emails and sends real-time transaction notifications to designated channels.

The bot connects to an email server using IMAP and listens for new payment confirmation emails as they arrive. When one comes in, it parses the email body to extract transaction details like the amount, sender name, bank reference number, and timestamp.

Once parsed, it formats everything into a clean Discord embed message and posts it to specific channels so the team can see payments come in without having to check email. It handles different email formats from various banks and payment providers, and has error handling for connection drops and malformed emails.

Olivia 2023 Website

The official website for OLIVIA 2023, a national vocational championship with 1,000+ students from across Indonesia.

OLIVIA 2023 (Olimpiade Vokasi Indonesia) is a national-scale academic competition hosted by Universitas Sebelas Maret. The event gathered over 1,000 vocational students from more than 70 universities across 16 provinces in Indonesia for various competition categories.

I designed and built the full website from scratch. It covered everything the event needed: detailed competition schedules, online registration flow, venue and accommodation info, category breakdowns, and a FAQ section. The site was fully responsive so participants could access it from their phones during the event.
